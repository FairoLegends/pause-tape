PAUSE TAPE

A VHS-styled app for pausing a project cleanly: record a short "tape" to your future self when you stop, and play it back when you return so you know exactly what to do next.

The Unique Kernel

Pausing a project becomes recording a message from your past self. When you come back, you play the tape on a CRT-style screen, and it hands you one small first step (about 10 minutes) with a timer, so you start working right away instead of staring and wandering. The app also records how many minutes it took you to actually get going again.

Who It's For

Anyone with a side project they've been forced to put on hold.

The first real user is the learner. They're freezing their horror game NO SIGNAL until 10 January 2027 for this hackathon, and they're afraid of coming back to forgotten progress, forgotten decisions, and no idea where to start.

Today: context is scattered across memory, a GDD, GitHub issue.md files, and their agents.

Why This Matters to the Learner

"Yang paling bikin aku semangat adalah momen saat aku kembali ke project game-ku nanti dan langsung tahu harus ngapain, bukan bengong dan mikir ke arah yang tidak jelas."

They'll use it for real: a NO SIGNAL tape, due 10 January 2027.

The Core Loop

The four fields recorded on every tape:

  1. Where I stopped
  2. First step when I return (small, ~10 min)
  3. What I'm still unsure about
  4. Why this project matters to me

Locked tapes can still be played early after a confirmation: "so it still feels like a message from the past, but doesn't block me if I can come back sooner." The calendar reminder is what brings people back.

Inspiration & Identity

Visually connected to NO SIGNAL, which has its own VHS recording system. Old CRT television, long scanlines, playing an old cassette, the era before smartphones.

"Seperti menerima pesan dari diriku sendiri di masa lalu, yang masih struggle cara menggunakan AI agent."

Should feel unique, not like a generic productivity app. App interface language: English.
