    HOW IT WORKS

    The room is built in code with three.js: the furniture, the light, the shadows and the sounds. Only the flat pictures are AI-generated, by Seedream 5.0 pro (dola-seedream-5-0-pro-260628, BytePlus), reached through an OpenAI-compatible API gateway, with the visible AI watermark switched off. No people, logos or text were asked for.

    Window view (day)

    Stylized semi-cartoon illustration for a cozy indie video game: the view from a ground-floor window straight across a quiet neighborhood street in a 1990s Indonesian housing complex, side-on across the road: a cream house with a red clay-tile roof and a garden wall with an iron gate, a big round mango tree, a peach house with a carport, a mint-green house, two coconut palms and a wooden utility pole. Chunky simplified shapes, soft cel shading, warm pastel colors, like a hand-painted animated-film background. Daytime: bright blue sky with a few soft white clouds, warm sunlight. No people, no vehicles, no text, no logos, no watermark.

    Window view (night)

    Not generated: a small script recolours the day picture to moonlight, draws a night sky with stars and lights the house windows and a street lamp, so every house and tree stays in place. The moon is drawn by the room.

    Painting: rice terraces

    A painting for a cozy semi-cartoon living room: stylized, chunky simplified shapes, soft painterly brush texture, gentle cel shading, warm muted pastel colors; no frame, signature, text, people or watermark. Subject: Indonesian rice terraces (sawah) in the morning, curved green terraces stepping down a hill, a small wooden hut, a few coconut palms, soft mist, a pale blue sky.

    Painting: volcano

    Same style. Subject: a calm volcano mountain in Java seen across green fields and a winding river, soft clouds around the peak, warm late-afternoon light.

    Painting: flowers

    Same style. Subject: a still life of tropical flowers in a simple clay vase on a table: frangipani, hibiscus and jasmine, with a plain warm background. Portrait.
